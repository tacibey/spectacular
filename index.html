FAQ

Asked, answered.

What exactly do I get each month?

Strategy one-pager, one hero film (30s–2min in 3 formats), 3 hook variants, banner set, ad copy pack and a testing plan. Delivered in 14 days with one revision round.

Is ad spend included?

No. $2,000 / $1,500 is the creative retainer. You (or your affiliate's Ad Credit) fund Meta spend separately through Whop Ads.

How does the $500/mo affiliate commission work?

Flat $500 per referred brand, every month they stay a paying client (recurring commission set up as a custom affiliate in Whop). Whop pays ~30 days after each client payment. Refunds, disputes or fraud claw the commission back. No sub-affiliates.

How does Ads For Affiliates work, technically?

You buy Ad Credit on Whop (min $50), tell us which of our 3 films to run, and we launch it from our Whop Ads agency account (Meta, managed agency access — no separate ad account needed) with your referral link as destination. We send weekly reports. V1 is concierge-run; a self-serve dashboard comes later.

Can I cancel?

Yes, monthly. Founding $1,500 locks only while you stay subscribed.

Who owns the creatives?

You do — full buyout for paid + organic use, forever, once paid.
